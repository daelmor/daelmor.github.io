> At Hugo Technologies, I played a key role in designing and enhancing backend systems for the **Delivery Services** platform, a core component of Hugo’s app ecosystem. I worked closely with cross-functional teams to build scalable, reliable solutions that supported the company’s fast-growing delivery operations across Central America and the Caribbean.

## **Key features**

-   **Order Management System (OMS):** Designed and optimized backend systems to handle order placement, tracking, and fulfillment at scale, ensuring seamless end-to-end operations for users, drivers, and merchants.
-   **Real-Time Dispatching:** Developed efficient algorithms for real-time assignment of delivery requests to drivers, balancing workload, delivery times, and customer satisfaction.
-   **Geo-Based Solutions:** Implemented geospatial tools and services for route optimization, distance calculation, and dynamic pricing based on delivery zones.
-   **Integrations:** Collaborated with external vendors and internal teams to integrate third-party APIs and payment gateways, enhancing the platform’s functionality and reliability.
-   **Performance Optimization:** Proactively identified and resolved bottlenecks, improving system performance to handle peak traffic during high-demand periods.

## **Key Achievements**

-   **Designed and developed scalable delivery services APIs** for enterprise clients, using Node.js, Parse Server, and TypeScript, improving delivery request response times by **35%** and supporting **100,000+ concurrent requests** during peak hours.
-   **Improved driver assignment efficiency** by **30%** using optimized **route-matching algorithms** and asynchronous task queues powered by Redis and background workers in Node.js.
-   Enhanced **delivery scheduling capabilities**, increasing daily deliveries by **15%** by introducing **cron-based background jobs** for scheduling recurring orders and reminders.
-   **Mentored cross-platform engineers**, conducting workshops on **RESTful API development**, **TypeScript best practices**, and **mobile API integrations**, contributing to a **25% improvement in code quality and productivity**.

## **Impact**  

My contributions significantly improved Hugo’s delivery efficiency, scalability, and user experience. By building and optimizing critical backend services, I ensured the platform could meet the demands of a growing customer base while maintaining a high standard of service reliability.

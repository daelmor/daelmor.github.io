Guarded by a handful of elite soldiers, the hidden city shelters the most precious of the Stone of Light, a legendary stone of magical power. When a devious military officer schemes to infiltrate the city and capture the sacred gem, his subversive plot is thwarted by an unlikely a desert wanderer who falls into a maze of treachery and betrayal, desire and danger...
